# Proposal: frontend-extension-motivetodo-rebrand

## Goal
To evolve the project's identity from the generic "Focus Todo" to the innovative, pun-based brand **MotiveTODO** and consolidate the dashboard headers to maximize vertical workspace.

## Scope
- **Rebrand**:
  - Update all "Focus Todo" text references to **"MotiveTODO"**.
  - Style the `TODO` portion of the header in your primary accent color for visual impact and branding.
- **Header Consolidation**:
  - Remove the redundant `<h2>Your Tasks</h2>` section.
  - Move the "Full Screen" and "Refresh" action buttons to the top `Dashboard` header.
- **Visual Polish**:
  - Ensure the new integrated header remains balanced and glassmorphic on both popup and full-tab views.

## Motivation
"Focus Todo" is a descriptor; **MotiveTODO** is a brand. By playing on the "Motivation to do" pun, we create a more active and engaging relationship with the user. Consolidating the headers reduces UI clutter and keeps the user's primary focus on their actual task list rather than navigation.
